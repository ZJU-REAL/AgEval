## Why trajectories

Failure analysis, multi-backend comparison, and training pipelines need **exportable runtime facts**. BORA treats Attempt evidence (including trajectory) as a product deliverable.

## What it is not

- Trajectory is **not** a PASS source  
- Missing trajectory must **not** invent PASS  
- Secrets / tokens must **not** enter evidence
