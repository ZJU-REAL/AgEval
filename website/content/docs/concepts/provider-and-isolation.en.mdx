## Two levels

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **L0 host** | Local process path; good for development and some public smokes |
| **L1 Docker** | Attempt runs in a container; mounts, UID, and network are enforced by Provider |

## Visibility projection

Gold and evaluator-only material stay unmounted until barrier materialize. Deleting YAML fields is not physical isolation.

Multi-actor L1 may declare `shared-container` or `container-per-group`. YAML owns logical topology only; container ids and UIDs belong to Runtime.
